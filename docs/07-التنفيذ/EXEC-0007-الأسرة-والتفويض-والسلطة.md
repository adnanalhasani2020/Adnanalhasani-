## Fixed implementation
Family Relationship، Delegation، Authorization Grant، Authority Policy، مع إبقاء Role Assignment ضمن Activities.
Family Relationship ≠ Delegation.
Delegation ≠ Authorization Grant.
Family Relationship ≠ Authorization.
Authorization Grant ≠ Role Assignment.

Delegation is contextual and scoped; it does not create Authorization Grant automatically.
Authorization Grant has explicit lifecycle states and does not transfer Domain Ownership.
Authority Policy remains distinct from Grant.
Agent Action and Approval remain distinct and cannot self-authorize.

## Explicit exclusions
No Parent, Child, Guardian, Minor, Consent, or Permission Role concept.
No automatic family authorization.
No legal eligibility rule.
No transfer of Financial Account, Clinical Record, Patient Context, Activity, Membership, or Role Assignment ownership.
